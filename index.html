1. Introduction

Welcome to BrainRot Translator ("the App"), developed and maintained by Raza ("we", "us", or "our"). BrainRot Translator is a real-time AI-powered speech and text translator designed to bridge the generational communication gap between millennial/older parents and Gen-Z / Gen-Alpha youth. The App provides bi-directional slang-to-standard English translation, slang severity controls, regional dialect adaptations (US, UK, India, Australia), comprehensive slang dictionary reference, offline conversation archives, and gamified coolness/aura achievements.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you install and use BrainRot Translator on your Android device, including information related to third-party services like Google AdMob and the Google Gemini AI API.

Key Summary: We do not sell your personal data. Audio recordings are processed solely in temporary memory to generate transcripts and are deleted immediately thereafter. All conversation histories and coolness stats are stored locally on your device in your private local database.

2. Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Audio & Voice Input (Microphone)

The App includes an AI voice translation feature. When you tap the microphone button, the App requests runtime permission to access your device microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO).

  • Purpose: Used exclusively to capture your spoken phrase so the AI engine can transcribe and translate it in real-time.
  • Processing & Ephemeral Storage: Audio is recorded into a temporary temporary cache file on your device and sent to the secure AI processing service. As soon as the transcription and translation response are received, the temporary audio file is destroyed.
  • No Voice Profiling: We do not create biometric voice prints, store voice profiles, or use your audio for advertising or third-party tracking.

B. Text Translation Inputs

When you manually type words, phrases, or slang into the translation console, the text is securely transmitted to the AI translation engine (Google Gemini API) to generate contextual parent/teen interpretations, breakdown terms, and example usages.

C. Local Conversation History & Game Statistics

Translations, favorite terms, coolness score, streaks, and unlocked achievement badges are persisted strictly locally on your device using an embedded SQLite Room database (AppDatabase).

  • This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any remote private cloud server.
  • You have 100% control over this data: you can clear individual conversations or use the "Clear All Logs" button inside the App at any time to wipe your entire history.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep BrainRot Translator free and accessible to all users, the App integrates and displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service offered by Google LLC.

We utilize various Google AdMob ad formats, including:

1. Banner Ads

Standard rectangular display ads appearing at the top or bottom of the screen during navigation.

2. Interstitial Ads

Full-screen ads appearing at natural transition pauses (e.g., after completing a translation session).

3. Rewarded Video Ads

Optional video advertisements that users may voluntarily choose to watch in exchange for bonus Coolness/Aura points or bonus badges.

4. Native / App Open Ads

Ads designed to match the visual look and feel of the app or shown during cold start launches.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain device information in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), subject to your Android device settings.
  • Device & Network Information: IP address, device model, operating system version, mobile carrier, language settings, and general approximate location (country/city level).
  • Ad Performance & Interaction Data: Impressions, click data, and diagnostic logs to measure ad efficacy and prevent fraud.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Used for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, and fraud prevention.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Artificial Intelligence Services (Google Gemini API)

The App utilizes Google's Gemini generative AI models to provide high-accuracy slang translations, slang tone adaptation, and regional youth colloquialism parsing.

  • Your input text and temporary audio payloads are communicated over encrypted HTTPS connections (TLS/SSL).
  • Transmitted input data is used strictly to fulfill your immediate real-time translation request.
  • Google's enterprise API terms prohibit the use of customer API prompts to train core public foundational models without user consent.

5. Android Device Permissions Required

Permission Type Justification & Usage
RECORD_AUDIO Runtime (Dangerous) Required to record your spoken phrase for real-time speech-to-slang translation. Only active when the user explicitly taps the microphone button.
INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Required to query the AI translation API and load advertisement inventory from Google AdMob.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Used by AdMob to verify whether the device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular network connection before attempting ad requests.

6. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA):

  • Local Data Deletion: You can wipe all stored translations, search logs, and saved items directly inside the App by tapping the "Clear All" button in the Conversation History screen.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the App from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes the local SQLite database and all cached data stored in the App's sandboxed storage.
  • Ad Personalization Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

BrainRot Translator is designed primarily for parents, teenagers, educators, and general audiences to understand contemporary youth linguistics. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). AdMob requests can be configured with child-directed treatment tags (tagForChildDirectedTreatment) where appropriate to ensure interest-based behavioral tracking is disabled for younger users.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable and industry-standard security precautions to protect your information:

  • All network transmissions to cloud AI endpoints are strictly enforced over Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / HTTPS).
  • App data is stored in private internal application storage, accessible only by the BrainRot Translator package.
  • No user accounts, passwords, or personal credentials are required to use the App.
